What we do

Services

Every package is built around protection and that wet-look finish — not a rushed rinse.

  • 01

    Signature wash & gloss

    Hand wash, safe drying, and a high-gloss finish that kills water spots and road film.

    • pH-balanced foam wash
    • Wheel & tyre clean
    • Plastic & trim dressing
    • Glass & mirror polish
  • 02

    Paint correction

    Machine polish to cut swirl marks, oxidation, and dull panels back to a deep reflection.

    • Single or multi-stage polish
    • Tank & fairing focus
    • Scratch reduction
    • Inspection under detail lighting
  • 03

    Ceramic / sealant protection

    Lock in the gloss with durable protection that sheds water and slows fade from sun and rain.

    • Surface prep & decontamination
    • Ceramic or polymer sealant
    • Hydrophobic top layer
    • Care guide after drop-off
  • 04

    Engine bay & drivetrain

    Degrease carefully, dress plastics, and leave the bay looking intentional — not neglected.

    • Safe degreasing
    • Chain clean & lube
    • Hardware wipe-down
    • Heat-safe dressings
  • 05

    Interior / seat care

    Seat, grips, and touch points cleaned and conditioned so the bike feels as sharp as it looks.

    • Seat clean & condition
    • Grip refresh
    • Instrument panel wipe
    • Storage compartment tidy
  • 06

    Show / sale prep

    Full visual reset for meets, shoots, or listing photos — maximum presence under any light.

    • End-to-end detailing
    • Chrome & metal brightening
    • Photo-ready finish
    • Optional same-day rush
